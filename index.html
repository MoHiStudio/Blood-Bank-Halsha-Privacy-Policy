Blood Bank Halsha

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Developer: MoHi Studio

Welcome to Blood Bank Halsha. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, when it is shared, and what choices you have when using the Blood Bank Halsha application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: name, email address, profile photo, and authentication identifier when you sign in, including through Google Sign-In.
  • Donor information: phone number, blood group, gender (if provided), district, upazila, address or location, donor availability, and donation history (if recorded).
  • Blood request information: requester or patient name, required blood group, hospital or location, required quantity, contact number, requested date and time, and other details entered in the request.
  • Technical information: app version, device or diagnostic information, and notification token, where required to operate the app and its notifications.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
  • Register and manage blood donors.
  • Process blood requests and connect requesters with relevant donors.
  • Send blood request notifications to donors whose registered blood group matches the requested blood group.
  • Allow eligible donors to view request details and contact the requester.
  • Maintain platform security, prevent misuse, and improve app functionality.

3. Blood Request Notifications

When a blood request is submitted, the app may identify registered donors with the matching blood group and send notifications to those donors only. A donor who opens the notification may view the request details made available by the app and use the provided contact information to contact the requester.

Notification delivery depends on device notification permissions, internet connectivity, and service availability. Users can disable notifications through their device settings.

4. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell or rent personal information. Some information may be displayed or shared within the app when necessary to provide its blood donation services. For example:

  • Donor profile details may be shown according to the app's donor visibility settings.
  • Blood request details may be made available to donors matching the requested blood group.
  • Requester contact details may be shown to eligible donors so they can respond to the request.

Information may also be processed by service providers that support app operations, such as Google and Firebase, or disclosed when required by law or necessary to address security, fraud, or safety concerns.

5. Google Sign-In

If Google Sign-In is enabled, Google may provide basic account information such as your name, email address, profile photo, and authentication-related identifier. The app does not receive your Google password.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored and processed using cloud services such as Firebase, depending on the app's implementation. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet legitimate operational needs, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us at the email address below. We will review and process valid requests, subject to information that must be retained for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law and the app's available features, you may request to:

  • Access or correct your account information.
  • Update your donor profile or availability.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal information.
  • Disable app notifications through your device settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Blood Bank Halsha is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

10. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Cloud Messaging, where implemented. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Firebase Privacy Information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at the URL where this policy is hosted. Please review it periodically.
